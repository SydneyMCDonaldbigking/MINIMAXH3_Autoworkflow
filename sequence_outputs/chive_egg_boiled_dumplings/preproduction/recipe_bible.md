# Chive and Egg Boiled Dumplings — No-Person Production Bible

## Food logic

Chop dry Chinese chives, scramble egg into small soft curds, season and mix immediately before wrapping. Fill and crimp thin dough rounds into crescents, then cook in actively boiling water until the dumplings float and look plump before lifting them out.

## Visual rule

No visible person. The commercial uses ingredients, cookware, steam and only cropped unidentifiable hands when a physical action must be shown. The opening reference uses the physical tabletop sign; H3 prompts do not name it.
