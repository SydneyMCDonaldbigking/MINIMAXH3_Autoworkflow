# Chive and Egg Boiled Dumplings — No-Person 3×5s Storyboard

Three independent 5-second H3 R2V clips. No people appear; only cropped anonymous hands may perform indispensable cooking actions.

## Clip 1: Fresh filling and wrapper setup

- Locked 45mm high three-quarter table shot. Begin on dry chopped chives, fluffy scrambled egg, thin wrappers and rolling pin in the bright northern kitchen. An anonymous hand cropped at the wrist folds the chives and egg together once with a wooden spoon, then leaves the glossy filling settled in the bowl. End with filling and wrappers clearly separated.
- Cut to a 70mm overhead insert. A small spoon places one measured portion of the green-and-yellow filling at the centre of a thin wheat wrapper. The spoon withdraws and the wrapper remains flat, with no other fillings entering frame. End ready for the crimping action.
- Cut to a 65mm board-level medium close shot. Two cropped anonymous hands fold the wrapper into one clean crescent and press a neat pleated seam, then release it onto the floured bamboo board. End on one intact raw dumpling among a few matching crescents.

## Clip 2: Crimped dumpling proof

- Locked 60mm board-level shot. Begin with several identical raw crescent dumplings neatly spaced on a floured bamboo board. Cropped anonymous hands lift one dumpling by the seam and set it down once, confirming the thin wrapper and sealed edge without tearing it.
- Cut to an 85mm macro insert. The pleated crescent seam and faint green-and-yellow filling silhouette stay crisp while a light dusting of flour settles on the board. No hands cross the frame after placement. End on the sealed dumpling texture.
- Cut to a medium pot-height shot. A slotted spoon lowers three raw dumplings together into clear rolling water in the black pot over blue flame. The dumplings enter once and the spoon stops; end at water entry under controlled steam.

## Clip 3: Rolling-boil to plated finish

- Locked 55mm pot shot. Begin on the clear rolling boil as pale dumplings float gently and become plump. A slotted spoon makes one slow lift beneath three dumplings, without breaking the water surface yet.
- Cut to an 80mm close insert. The spoon rises above the pot holding glossy intact dumplings while clear water drains back in thin streams. Steam stays behind the dumplings and does not obscure their crescent forms. End with the spoon held level.
- Cut to a calm 65mm plate hero. The spoon places the dumplings onto a shallow white plate beside black vinegar and chilli oil; one dumpling is already cut cleanly to show chives and egg. End with the plated dumplings sharp and appetising.
