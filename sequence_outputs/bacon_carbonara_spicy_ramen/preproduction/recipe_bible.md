## Recipe logic

Warm the supplied spicy sauce with milk or cooking cream and the retained noodle water over low heat, fold in the noodles and carbonara seasoning until every curl is glossy pale orange. Finish with cheese; the yolk stays intact on the plated noodles and is not scrambled into the sauce. The dish must be creamy and substantial rather than a red soup, with no fake cheese pull or excessive chilli flakes.
