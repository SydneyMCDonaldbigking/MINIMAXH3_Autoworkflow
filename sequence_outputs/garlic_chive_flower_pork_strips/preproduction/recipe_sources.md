Project: Garlic Chive Flower Pork Strips

Target: Australia / Instagram Reels material package.

Sources checked:

- Mammafong, Stir-Fried Pork with Garlic Chive Flower: https://mammafong.com/blogs/recipes/stir-fried-pork-with-garlic-chive-flower-%E9%9F%AD%E8%8F%9C%E8%8A%B1%E8%82%89%E7%B5%B2
- Epicurious, Flowering Chives and Pork Slivers: https://www.epicurious.com/recipes/food/views/flowering-chives-pork-slivers-betty-liu/

Working recipe direction:

- Pork loin or lean pork shoulder is cut into thin strips and lightly marinated.
- Marinade: light soy sauce, oyster sauce, Shaoxing wine, white pepper, cornstarch, a little neutral oil.
- Garlic chive flowers are cut into 5 cm lengths; green stems and unopened buds stay visually obvious.
- Stir-fry pork first, remove or push aside, then quickly fry aromatics and chive flowers.
- Combine with a light soy/oyster sauce mixture and serve immediately while chive flowers stay semi-crisp.
