# Recipe Bible

Project: Garlic Chive Flower Pork Strips

## Dish Logic

Home-style Chinese stir-fry built around green garlic chive flowers and tender pork strips. The video must prove the vegetable identity: long green hollow stems with unopened flower buds, cut into short lengths, then cooked briefly with glossy pork strips.

## Ingredients

- garlic chive flowers
- pork loin or lean pork shoulder, thin strips
- light soy sauce
- oyster sauce
- Shaoxing wine
- white pepper
- cornstarch
- neutral oil
- garlic and ginger
- optional red chilli

## Visual Rules

- Garlic chive flowers must stay green and show unopened flower buds.
- Pork must be thin strips, not minced pork, pork belly slabs, or chunks.
- Stir-fry should look glossy and lightly sauced, not soupy.
- The chive flowers should remain crisp-looking, not wilted into dark mush.
- Keep unrelated seafood, poultry, dumplings, and drink visuals out of this stir-fry sequence.

## Three Clip Chain

1. Garlic chive flowers and pork strips are prepared; pork is marinated and chives are cut into 5 cm segments.
2. Pork strips sear in a hot wok, then garlic/ginger and chive flowers enter for fast stir-frying.
3. Sauce coats the pork and chive flowers; final plated hero with rice.
