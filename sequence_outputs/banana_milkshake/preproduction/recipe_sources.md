# Recipe Sources

Project: Banana Milkshake

Target: Australia / Instagram Reels material package.

Sources checked:

- BBC Good Food, Banana Milkshake: https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/banana-milkshake
- Allrecipes, Banana Milkshake: https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/238627/banana-milkshake/

Working recipe direction:

- Ripe bananas are the main product cue.
- Blend banana with cold milk.
- Use vanilla ice cream or a small amount of honey for a creamier, sweeter visual.
- Ice cubes are optional for a cold, frothy Australian summer feel.
- Final colour should be pale creamy yellow, not orange or brown.
