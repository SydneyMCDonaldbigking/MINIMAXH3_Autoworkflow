# Recipe Bible

Project: Banana Milkshake

## Dish Logic

Fast chilled banana milkshake for Australia / Instagram. This is a simple one-setting drink ritual: bananas move from counter/fridge to blender, milk and a creamy sweet component go in, the blender turns the mixture into a thick pale-yellow shake, then the drink pours into a clear glass.

## Ingredients

- ripe yellow bananas
- cold milk
- vanilla ice cream or honey
- ice cubes
- optional banana slice garnish

## Visual Rules

- Banana identity must be obvious: yellow peel, pale banana flesh, curved banana pieces.
- Milkshake texture must be creamy, thick, lightly frothy, and pale yellow.
- No brown smoothie, no protein-shake powder, no labels, no fake brand packaging.
- Keep unrelated seafood, poultry, dumplings, and soup out of this drink sequence.
- AGO/UMALL may appear only as a small real prop in the opening if needed; no floating logo.

## Three Clip Chain

1. Bananas and cold ingredients set up at the kitchen counter; banana is peeled and cut into the blender.
2. Milk, ice, and vanilla ice cream or honey enter; blender mixes into a smooth creamy swirl.
3. Shake pours into a clear glass, banana slice garnish lands, final chilled hero.
