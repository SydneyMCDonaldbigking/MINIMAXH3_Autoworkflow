# 印尼风味烤鸡腿 — 无真人制作圣经

## Recipe logic

Make ayam bakar directly on the charcoal grill, never in a wok or pot: marinate one bone-in chicken leg in a fragrant paste of shallot, garlic, ginger, turmeric, coriander, bruised lemongrass and sweet soy sauce. Put it directly over calm medium charcoal, turn it through the grill cycle until fully cooked, then brush only thin coats of sweet-soy glaze until the skin turns deep mahogany with controlled dark char at a few edges. The chicken must be fully cooked, never pink, raw, breaded, blackened all over or drowned in sauce.
