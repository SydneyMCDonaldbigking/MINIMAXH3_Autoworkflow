# Recipe Bible

Dish name: Japanese Teriyaki Chicken Thigh Rice

Core ingredients:

- Chicken thigh, preferably skin-on boneless thigh for pan sear and whole-thigh plating.
- Soy sauce, sake, mirin, sugar.
- Optional ginger for aroma.
- Steamed short-grain rice.
- Scallion, toasted sesame, cooked broccoli.

Visual chain:

1. Product/prep hook: chicken thigh and teriyaki sauce components on a clean Australian apartment kitchen counter.
2. Heat proof: chicken thigh sears in a pan, then glossy teriyaki sauce bubbles and coats the surface.
3. Hero payoff: one whole intact teriyaki chicken thigh beside steamed rice and cooked broccoli, with sauce drizzle, sesame, and scallion.

H3 reference requirements:

- Raw-state reference: pale raw chicken thigh, skin texture, sauce kit.
- Cook-state reference: glossy browned thigh in bubbling teriyaki glaze.
- Hero-state reference: whole teriyaki chicken thigh rice bowl with cooked broccoli.
- Keep the dish separate from freezer shrimp wonton soup and unrelated seafood material.

Negative identity notes:

- No unrelated raw fish, no wontons, no soup.
- Do not turn the chicken into wings, breast strips, nuggets, or fried chicken.
- Do not slice, cut open, or expose the chicken interior in the final hero.
- Do not create readable labels, fake prices, subtitles, overlays, or floating logos.
