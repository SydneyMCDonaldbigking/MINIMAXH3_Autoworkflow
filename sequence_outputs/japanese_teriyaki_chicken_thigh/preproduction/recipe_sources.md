# Recipe Sources

Dish: Japanese Teriyaki Chicken Thigh Don

Target: Australia / Instagram Reels / MiniMax H3 3 x 5s Ref2VA.

Primary source:

- Just One Cookbook, "Chicken Teriyaki": https://www.justonecookbook.com/chicken-teriyaki/

Useful recipe logic:

- Use chicken thighs as the hero protein.
- Japanese teriyaki is a cooking method: pan-fry, then reduce a glossy tare in the pan.
- Sauce logic: soy sauce, sake, mirin, sugar, close to a 2:2:2:1 ratio.
- Score/flatten thigh for even thickness, sear, steam/cover briefly, then glaze and spoon sauce until shiny.
- Serve with steamed rice and a simple green side.

Commercial adaptation:

- Make it a rice bowl for IG clarity: steamed rice, sliced glossy teriyaki chicken thigh, sesame, scallion, cucumber or broccoli.
- Keep raw chicken only in prep references and clip 01. From clip 02 onward, chicken must be cooked and glossy.
- No bottled teriyaki sauce; show sauce ingredients in small bowls with no readable labels.
