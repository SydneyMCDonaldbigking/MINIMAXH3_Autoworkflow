# Recipe Bible: Cucumber Egg Soup

## Production Use

Classic home-style cucumber egg soup for a short vertical cooking commercial.
Use the supplied cucumber photo as the product identity: long dark green bumpy
cucumbers, not luffa or zucchini.

## Visual Food States

- Product reference: fresh long cucumbers with bumpy dark green skin.
- Ingredient kit: cucumbers, eggs, beaten egg, scallion, ginger optional, white pepper, salt, sesame oil, light stock.
- Character scene: new no-face cook and new kitchen, different from the prior white apartment kitchen.
- Prep state: thin cucumber half-moons or ribbons, beaten egg, scallion.
- Cook state: cucumber pieces in light broth with soft yellow egg ribbons.
- Hero state: clear light cucumber egg soup, green cucumber and yellow egg visible.

## Commercial Beat Chain

1. Clip 01: wash/slice cucumber and beat eggs.
2. Clip 02: simmer cucumber briefly and slowly drizzle egg.
3. Clip 03: ladle soup, garnish, final bowl hero.

## Motion Rules

Use locked camera and slow action. No fast chopping, no rapid stirring, no hard
boil, no busy hand motion. The egg pour should be a thin steady stream with a
short pause after ribbons form.
