## Creative Rules

- Classic home-style cucumber egg soup: long dark green cucumber, egg ribbons, scallion, light clear broth.
- Keep it cucumber, not luffa, zucchini, bitter melon, tomato soup, noodle soup, or dumpling soup.
- Three clips: slow cucumber/egg prep, gentle simmer and egg ribbon pour, ladle and final bowl.
- New kitchen/person only for this dish: sage-green top, black waist apron, dark walnut kitchen.
- Motion rule: one low ingredient slide, one slow stir, one thin steady egg pour, one ladle pour. No hard boiling, rapid stirring, fast chopping, or busy camera.
