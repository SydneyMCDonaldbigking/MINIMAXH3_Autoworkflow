## Production Facts Extracted

- Cucumber egg soup is a light quick soup built from sliced or shaved cucumber and beaten egg.
- Common steps: slice cucumber, beat egg, bring water or stock to a gentle boil, add cucumber briefly, drizzle egg in a thin stream, season with salt/white pepper/sesame oil/scallion.
- Egg ribbons are clearer when the soup is at a gentle simmer and the egg is poured slowly.
- Cucumber should remain fresh green and not cook to a dull mush.
- Motion should stay slow: slice, show still prep, gentle simmer, thin egg pour, ladle, final bowl.
