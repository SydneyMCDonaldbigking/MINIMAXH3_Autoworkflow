# Recipe Sources

Dish chosen: Freezer Shrimp Wonton Egg Drop Soup.

Decision: I chose a wonton egg drop soup because it fits the user's requested action chain: take frozen wontons from the freezer, add water, add soup seasoning, cook, and finish with egg. Raw chicken thigh references are saved as separate material references, but they are not staged inside this soup route.

## Sources Used

1. The Woks of Life, "Wonton Egg Drop Soup"  
   https://thewoksoflife.com/wonton-egg-drop-soup/  
   Production facts used: frozen wontons can be the shortcut; keep wontons frozen until cooking; egg drop soup is a quick golden broth; turmeric can help the yellow restaurant-style colour; scallions are enough for a clean base.

2. Omnivore's Cookbook, "Chinese Egg Drop Soup"  
   https://omnivorescookbook.com/chinese-egg-drop-soup/  
   Production facts used: simmer chicken broth with green onion and ginger; use chicken bouillon for body; white pepper, turmeric, cornstarch slurry, beaten egg, and sesame oil give the classic flavour/texture; add egg into an active low simmer for ribbons.

3. Food Network Kitchen, "Chicken Soup with Dumplings"  
   https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/food-network-kitchen/chicken-soup-with-dumplings-8874813  
   Production facts used: frozen dumplings or mini wontons can simmer directly in broth; ginger, garlic, scallions, soy sauce, bok choy, and sesame oil are compatible fast-soup flavour cues.

4. Food Standards Australia New Zealand, "Food safety standards - temperature control requirements"  
   https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/business/safety/factsheets/codestandardst857  
   Production facts used: potentially hazardous foods need temperature control at 5 C or colder, or 60 C or hotter. This supports the freezer/fridge handling visuals.

5. NSW Food Authority, "Cooking temperatures"  
   https://www.foodauthority.nsw.gov.au/consumer/food-at-home/cooking-temperatures  
   Production facts used: Australia-facing safety anchor for raw materials: fish 63 C, chicken/turkey 74 C. This is why unrelated raw fish or raw chicken thigh are not inserted into a fast 15s soup story.

6. FoodSafety.gov, "Cook to a Safe Minimum Internal Temperature"  
   https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures  
   Production facts used: eggs should be cooked until firm/set; shellfish should become pearly or white and opaque; poultry needs 74 C. This shapes the final visual endpoint.

## Distilled Production Facts

- Main product: frozen shrimp wontons/dumplings in ordinary unbranded packaging.
- Soup base: water plus a visual seasoning kit of chicken bouillon powder, white pepper, tiny turmeric, cornflour slurry, ginger, scallion, and sesame oil.
- Fast route: keep wontons frozen until pot time; bring water/seasoning to a boil; add frozen wontons; simmer until wrappers turn translucent and filling is opaque; drizzle beaten egg into a low simmer for ribbons.
- Visual endpoint: clear golden broth, soft translucent wonton skins, visible shrimp-pink filling, pale yellow egg ribbons, scallion rings, a few dark garnish strips, steam.
- Safety direction for visuals: do not show raw chicken or unrelated raw fish going into this quick soup; any separate raw-protein ad needs its own cook-state and endpoint references.
