# Recipe Bible

## Production Use

Create a 3x5s MiniMax H3 Ref2VA cooking commercial for Australia / Instagram Reels. The ad sells a quick freezer-to-bowl meal: frozen shrimp wontons become a golden egg-drop soup with a simple soup seasoning kit.

## Real Recipe Basis

The route combines frozen wonton soup and egg drop soup. Sources support frozen wontons cooked from frozen, chicken stock or bouillon as the base, scallion/ginger/white pepper/sesame oil for flavour, turmeric for gentle golden colour, cornstarch for body, and beaten egg drizzled into a low simmer for ribbons.

## Concise Recipe Plan

Keep the frozen wontons frozen until the pot is ready. Bring water and soup seasoning to a boil: chicken bouillon or stock powder, ginger, scallion whites, white pepper, a tiny pinch of turmeric, and sesame oil. Add frozen shrimp wontons and simmer until the wrappers turn translucent and the filling reads opaque. Lower to a gentle simmer, stir in a cornflour slurry for body, then drizzle beaten egg slowly to form ribbons. Finish with scallion greens and a few dark garnish strips.

- Product state: ordinary unbranded frozen shrimp wonton pack, pale frozen dumplings visible through a frosty opening or on a tray.
- Egg state: brown eggs, one cracked egg, beaten egg in a small bowl ready for ribbons.
- Seasoning state: small bowls of chicken bouillon powder, white pepper, turmeric, cornflour slurry, ginger slices, scallion, sesame oil.
- Prep state: freezer pack on counter, pot of clear water, seasoning kit, beaten egg, scallions ready.
- Cook state: golden broth at a simmer, frozen wontons becoming glossy and translucent, egg ribbons entering the broth.
- Hero state: finished bowl, shrimp wontons intact, pale yellow egg ribbons, scallion greens, clear golden broth, steam.
