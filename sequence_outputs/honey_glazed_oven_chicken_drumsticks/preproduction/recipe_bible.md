# Honey Glazed Oven-Baked Chicken Drumsticks

## Production Use

This recipe bible is for a vertical food commercial built around raw skin-on chicken drumsticks, honey-soy marinade, and an oven-baked glossy finish.

## Real Recipe Basis

The cooking logic is based on common oven-baked honey soy drumstick methods:

- Score or slash skin-on drumsticks so marinade reaches the meat.
- Use a sweet-salty marinade built from honey, soy sauce, garlic, ginger, oil, and a small acidic note such as lemon juice or rice vinegar.
- Marinate for at least 2 hours, ideally overnight.
- Bake in a hot oven around 190-200 C / 375-400 F.
- Turn or baste during baking so the glaze becomes sticky and amber.
- Cook poultry to at least 74 C / 165 F in the thickest part, avoiding the bone.

## Recipe

Serves: 3-4

Ingredients:

- 6 skin-on chicken drumsticks
- 3 tbsp honey
- 3 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 3 garlic cloves, finely minced
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, finely grated
- 1 tbsp lemon juice or rice vinegar
- 1/2 tsp Chinese five-spice powder
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- Toasted sesame seeds and sliced spring onion, for garnish

Method:

1. Pat the drumsticks dry. Cut 2-3 shallow diagonal slashes across the thickest part of each drumstick.
2. Mix honey, soy sauce, oyster sauce, neutral oil, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, lemon juice or rice vinegar, five-spice, and black pepper into a glossy marinade.
3. Coat the drumsticks thoroughly, rubbing glaze into the slashes and over the skin. Marinate at least 2 hours, ideally overnight.
4. Preheat the oven to 200 C / 400 F. Line a tray with baking paper or foil.
5. Place the drumsticks skin-side up in a single layer. Bake for 20 minutes.
6. Turn the drumsticks, brush with marinade or tray glaze, and bake for another 15 minutes.
7. Brush again and bake 5-10 minutes more, until the skin is deep amber, sticky, and lightly charred at the edges.
8. Check the thickest part reaches at least 74 C / 165 F, avoiding the bone. Rest for 5 minutes.
9. Finish with sesame seeds and sliced spring onion.

- Product state: six pale pink skin-on drumsticks on a white plate, skin texture visible, raw flesh visible near the cut ends.
- Prep state: scored drumsticks coated in dark honey-soy glaze, garlic and ginger clinging to the skin.
- Oven state: drumsticks arranged in one layer on a lined metal tray, glaze bubbling at the edges, skin tightening.
- Baste state: a brush drags thick amber glaze over browned skin.
- Hero state: glossy amber-brown drumsticks with sticky lacquered skin, sesame seeds, spring onion, steam, and a small glaze puddle on the tray.
