# Recipe Source Notes

## Better Homes & Gardens Australia

URL: https://www.bhg.com.au/recipe/food/baked-chicken-drumsticks-with-ginger-honey-lemon-and-soy/

Useful production facts:

- Uses chicken drumsticks with honey, soy sauce, lemon juice, ginger, sesame oil, and Chinese five spice.
- Cuts small parallel lines in each drumstick before baking.
- Bakes at 180 C fan-forced / 200 C conventional.
- Uses repeated basting and finishes when a probe thermometer reads 75 C.

## Jo Cooks

URL: https://www.jocooks.com/recipes/honey-soy-chicken-drumsticks/

Useful production facts:

- Uses bone-in, skin-on chicken drumsticks.
- Marinade logic: honey, low-sodium soy sauce, garlic, ginger, olive oil, sesame oil, and pepper.
- Recommends marinating for at least 2 hours, overnight preferred.
- Bakes at 375 F and turns drumsticks halfway through for even color.

## USDA FSIS

URL: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart

Useful production fact:

- Poultry should reach a safe minimum internal temperature of 165 F / 74 C.

## Prompt Implications

- The visual story should not imply raw chicken becomes finished instantly.
- The prep state must show slashes and marinade coating before the oven.
- The oven state must show real heat: tray, oven rack, orange oven light, bubbling glaze, and tightening skin.
- The final hero should be sticky, amber, and lacquered, not pale, boiled, fried, or saucy like a stew.
