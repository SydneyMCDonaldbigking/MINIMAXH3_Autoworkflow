Sticky honey-soy chicken drumsticks, baked in the oven until the glaze turns glossy and amber.

The trick is simple: score the skin, marinate well, bake hot, and brush again near the end so the honey-soy glaze catches the heat.

Recipe idea:
6 chicken drumsticks
Honey
Soy sauce
Oyster sauce
Garlic
Ginger
Sesame oil
Lemon juice or rice vinegar
Chinese five spice

Bake at 200 C / 400 F until the thickest part reaches 74 C / 165 F, then finish with sesame seeds and spring onion.

Save this for an easy oven dinner.
