Thai volcano pork ribs, simmered for an hour and stacked into a cone before the sauce ever touches them.

This one is not fried. The ribs go into cold water with bruised lemongrass, ginger, spring onion and coriander root, and simmer covered until the meat shrinks back off the bone. The heat is all in the dressing, and the dressing is raw.

Recipe idea:
Pork ribs, cut into 10 cm lengths
Lemongrass, bruised
Ginger, sliced
Spring onion, bruised
Coriander root
Salt
For the dressing: bird's eye chilli, garlic, palm sugar, lime juice, fish sauce, shallot, coriander

Simmer covered for at least an hour, until the meat pulls back from the bone ends. Pork should reach 75 C. Blend the dressing raw and pour it over the stack just before serving.

Save this for the next time you want something loud.
