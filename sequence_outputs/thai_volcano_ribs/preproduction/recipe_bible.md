# Thai Volcano Pork Ribs (泰式火山排骨, Leng Saap / เล้งแซ่บ)

## Production Use

This recipe bible is for a vertical food commercial built around raw pork ribs, a
long aromatic simmer, and a raw chilli-lime dressing poured over a stacked cone.

**Read this before rewriting any prompt for this dish.** Our first version of this
ad deep fried the ribs. That is a different dish. Leng Saap is a boiled-rib dish
and the entire visual grammar - pale meat, steam, no oil, no crust - follows from
that.

## Real Recipe Basis

- Rub the ribs with corn starch and salt and soak in water about 20 minutes to
  draw out blood, then rinse.
- Start them in **cold** water, not boiling, with bruised aromatics.
- Aromatics are lemongrass, ginger, spring onion and coriander root. They are
  bruised, not chopped, so they perfume the broth without clouding it with debris.
- Simmer covered on medium heat for **at least an hour**, until the meat visibly
  shrinks back from the bone ends.
- The dressing is blended **raw** - no oil, no heat, no thickener - and poured
  from the top down over the stacked ribs at the moment of serving.
- Pork should reach 75 C internally; a covered hour clears that comfortably.

## Recipe

Serves: 3-4

Ingredients:

- 900 g-1 kg meaty pork ribs, cut into lengths about 10 cm
- 2 stalks lemongrass, bruised flat and cut into lengths
- 1 thumb ginger, sliced into thick coins
- 2 spring onions, bruised along their length
- 15 g coriander root, washed
- 1 tsp salt
- Water to cover, roughly 2.5-3 L
- For the dressing: 6-10 bird's eye chillies, 4 garlic cloves, 2 tbsp palm sugar,
  4 tbsp lime juice, 3 tbsp fish sauce, 1 shallot, a handful of coriander
- To finish: fresh coriander, sliced red chilli, lime wedges

Method:

1. Rub the ribs with corn starch and salt, soak in cold water 20 minutes, rinse.
2. Put the ribs in a tall pot and cover with **cold** water. Add the bruised
   lemongrass, ginger, spring onion, coriander root and salt.
3. Bring to the boil, skim, then cover and simmer on medium heat for at least an
   hour. The broth turns cloudy and the meat pulls back off the bone ends.
4. Blend all dressing ingredients raw into a loose bright red-orange sauce. Do not
   cook it.
5. Lift the ribs out. Stand them up against each other, bone ends outward, into a
   tall cone on a dark platter.
6. Pour the dressing over the peak so it runs down every side. Scatter coriander
   and sliced chilli, and set lime wedges at the base.

## Visual Food States

- Product state: raw pork ribs in a black retail tray, pale pink meat with clean
  white bone ends. **Frame the meat and bare tray rim only** - the printed label
  and barcode stay out of shot.
- Prep state: ribs in a colander beside bruised lemongrass lengths, thick ginger
  coins, bruised spring onion and washed coriander root. No sauce, no colour on
  the meat.
- Simmer state: a tall stockpot, ribs submerged in cloudy pale broth moving at a
  slow lazy roll, aromatics turning among them, steam sitting over the surface,
  lid to one side. **No oil anywhere.**
- Lift state: tongs holding one rib clear of the broth, meat sagging off the bone,
  broth running off in a thin stream. Nothing browned or crisp.
- Hero state: ribs stacked into a tall cone, meat pale and shrunk back from clean
  white bone ends, bright red-orange dressing running down the outside, coriander
  and chilli over the top, lime at the base.

## Commercial Beat Chain

Clip 01: product hook on the counter, aromatics bruised and gathered, ribs and
aromatics lowered together into a pot of **cold** water. The pot is not yet lit.

Clip 02: the lid lifts in one movement and releases a wave of steam, the long
simmer is revealed, the broth clouds and the meat draws back from the bone, tongs
lift one rib clear.

Clip 03: ribs stood up into the volcano cone, the raw dressing poured from the
peak so it runs down every side, one rib pulled from the top of the stack.
