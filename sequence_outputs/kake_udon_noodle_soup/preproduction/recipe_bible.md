# Recipe Bible

Project: Classic Kake Udon Noodle Soup

## Dish Logic

Classic Japanese kake udon: chewy thick udon noodles in a clear amber dashi broth. This is not stir-fried yaki udon and not ramen. The visual transformation is frozen udon package to loosened glossy noodles, then hot dashi poured over the noodles in a bowl.

## Ingredients

- frozen udon noodles
- dashi broth, made from kombu and katsuobushi or dashi powder
- light soy sauce or usukuchi soy sauce
- mirin
- optional sake and a pinch of sugar
- sliced green onion
- kamaboko or narutomaki fish cake
- tempura flakes
- shichimi togarashi

## Visual Rules

- Udon must be thick, white, glossy, and chewy-looking.
- Broth must be clear light amber, not creamy ramen broth.
- Keep the dish minimal and Japanese: scallion, fish cake, tempura flakes, shichimi.
- Do not show spaghetti, ramen, soba, stir-fry, heavy meat toppings, curry, or random vegetables.
- Packaging can be generic clear frozen udon packaging; do not reproduce the exact supplied brand artwork.

## Three Clip Chain

1. Frozen udon pack comes out, dashi seasonings are prepared, noodles are loosened.
2. Clear dashi broth simmers and hot udon noodles are transferred to the bowl.
3. Broth pours over the noodles; scallion, fish cake, tempura flakes, and shichimi finish the hero bowl.
