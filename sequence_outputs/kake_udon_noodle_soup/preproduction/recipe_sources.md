# Recipe Sources

Project: Classic Kake Udon Noodle Soup

Reference direction: classic Japanese hot udon, not yaki udon. Thick frozen udon is warmed separately, then served in a clear dashi broth seasoned with soy sauce and mirin. Simple toppings: sliced scallion, kamaboko or narutomaki, tempura flakes, and shichimi.

- Just One Cookbook, Udon Noodle Soup (Kake Udon): https://www.justonecookbook.com/udon-noodle-soup/
- Bon Appetit, Soothing Kake Udon: https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/kake-udon-noodle-soup
- Kyrié the Foodié, Udon Noodle Soup (Kake Udon): https://farmerxfoodie.com/udon-noodle-soup-kake-udon/
