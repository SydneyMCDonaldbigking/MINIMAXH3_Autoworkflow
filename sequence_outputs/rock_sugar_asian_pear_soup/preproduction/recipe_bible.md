# Recipe Bible

Project: Rock Sugar Asian Pear Soup

## Dish Logic

Classic Chinese-style rock sugar Asian pear dessert soup. The visual story is pear identity first, then hollowing and filling with rock sugar, red dates, goji berries, and snow fungus, then gentle steaming or simmering, then a translucent golden syrup hero.

## Ingredients

- Asian pear / snow pear
- yellow rock sugar
- red dates
- goji berries
- soaked snow fungus
- water

## Visual Rules

- Pear must look like the user reference: round Asian pear, light yellow skin, tiny speckles.
- Do not make a green western pear or sliced apple.
- Dessert liquid should be clear golden, not milky, oily, brown, or thick sauce.
- Red dates and goji berries are visible but not overwhelming.
- Snow fungus should look translucent and soft, not like cabbage or noodles.
- Keep unrelated seafood, poultry, dumplings, and drink visuals out of this dessert-soup sequence.

## Three Clip Chain

1. Asian pear and ingredients on counter; pear top is cut and core is removed.
2. Rock sugar, red dates, goji berries, and snow fungus go in; pear steams gently.
3. Lid opens, syrup is spooned, and final warm dessert soup hero lands.
