# Recipe Sources

Project: Rock Sugar Asian Pear Soup

Target: Australia / Instagram Reels material package.

Sources checked:

- Red House Spice, Rock Sugar Pear: https://redhousespice.com/rock-sugar-pear/
- The Woks of Life, Snow Fungus Soup with Pears: https://thewoksoflife.com/snow-fungus-soup-pears/
- Anncoo Journal, Rock Sugar Steamed Pear: https://www.anncoojournal.com/rock-sugar-steamed-pear/

Working recipe direction:

- Use Asian pear / snow pear visual identity: round, pale yellow, speckled skin.
- Hollow or cut the pear, then add yellow rock sugar.
- Red dates and goji berries provide red garnish contrast.
- Snow fungus is optional but useful for dessert-soup texture.
- Steam or gently simmer until pear softens and syrup turns clear-golden.
