# 家常煎蛋青菜汤面｜Recipe Bible

## Production use

One continuous 10-second vertical product-to-meal commercial for Havista Wife's Noodles.

## Cooking logic

1. Remove one compact noodle bundle from the opened package.
2. Lower the noodles into actively boiling clear water and let the strands loosen.
3. Blanch washed baby bok choy separately or immediately after the noodles.
4. Fry one egg until the white is fully opaque and firm and the yolk has begun to thicken.
5. Place drained noodles and bok choy in a clear light-golden broth, then top with the fried egg and sliced scallion.

## Visual state chain

- Product/opening: one authentic clear noodle package and one bundle moving upward.
- Prep: pale ivory noodle bundles, washed bok choy, scallion, one egg and clear water; no packaging.
- Boil: strands entering bubbling water and beginning to separate; bok choy waits outside the pot.
- Egg: one fried egg in a matte-black skillet, opaque set white and lightly crisp edge.
- Hero: clear broth, separate pale noodles, bright bok choy, one fried egg and restrained scallion.

## Commercial beat chain

`take one bundle from package → lower noodles into boiling water → fry one egg → place egg onto finished noodle-and-bok-choy bowl → hold hero`
