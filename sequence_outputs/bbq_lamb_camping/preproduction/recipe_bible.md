# 户外露营羊肉串 (Outdoor Camping Lamb Skewers) — Production Bible

## Production use

Commercial reference-generation package: three independent 5-second vertical MiniMax H3 Ref2VA clips, no final concat by default.

## Real basis

Thread alternating 3 cm lean-lamb cubes and white fat cubes onto thin skewers, seven to eight pieces each. Lay the skewers directly over glowing charcoal with no open flame, turning every two to three minutes until each side reaches an even sauce-brown colour. Dust with cumin and chilli flakes while turning near the end so the seasoning toasts onto the surface rather than burning. The fat should render to a glossy sheen and the lean meat should read fully opaque brown throughout with no pink centre, consistent with USDA guidance that whole-muscle lamb reach a minimum 145 degF (63 degC) internal temperature, taken further to a fully opaque well-done finish for pieces this small.

## Source list

- [烤羊肉串的做法——新疆味道](https://home.meishichina.com/recipe-107447.html) - 羊肉切3厘米见方、肥瘦相间穿串，无明火炭火烤制，肉色转酱黄色后翻面，烤制中撒孜然面和辣椒面。
- [USDA FSIS: Lamb From Farm to Table](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/meat-catfish/lamb-farm-table) - 整块羊肉（如烤串所用的小块）安全最低中心温度为145°F（63°C），静置至少3分钟。
