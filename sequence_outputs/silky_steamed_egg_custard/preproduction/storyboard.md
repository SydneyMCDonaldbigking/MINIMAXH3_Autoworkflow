# Silky Steamed Egg Custard — No-Person 3×5s Storyboard

Three independent 5-second H3 R2V clips. No people appear; only cropped anonymous hands may perform indispensable cooking actions.

## Clip 1: Egg, warm-water and soy setup

- Locked 45mm high three-quarter counter shot. Begin on cracked eggs in a clear bowl, warm water pitcher, light soy sauce pourer, fine sieve and white heatproof bowl. A cropped anonymous hand whisks the eggs once until the yolk and white combine, then leaves the whisk still.
- Cut to a 70mm close side insert. Warm water pours in a single thin stream while the whisk makes one slow circle, keeping the liquid pale golden and even. The light soy sauce pourer remains in view. End with smooth diluted egg mixture.
- Cut to a 65mm top three-quarter shot. Light soy sauce is added in one small pour and the mixture is guided toward the fine sieve. No foam, other seasonings or people enter. End ready to strain.

## Clip 2: Strain, cover and steam

- Locked 55mm close shot. Begin on the fine sieve over the white heatproof bowl. Pale egg mixture passes through in one smooth pour, leaving a bubble-free surface in the bowl. The sieve lifts away once and the surface stays level.
- Cut to an 80mm bowl-height insert. A lid or heat-safe cover settles over the bowl in one calm downward motion, with no water droplets inside. End on the covered bowl ready for gentle steam.
- Cut to a medium steamer shot. The covered bowl is placed inside a bamboo steamer above gentle steam; the lid closes once. Camera stays fixed, ending on the closed steamer and soft escaping vapour.

## Clip 3: Silky custard finish

- Locked 50mm steamer shot. Begin on the closed bamboo lid as soft steam rises. Anonymous hands lift the lid once and reveal a fully set, smooth pale-golden custard with a mirror-like surface, then hold the lid away.
- Cut to an 85mm macro insert. A small spoon drizzles light soy sauce and a few drops of sesame oil across the custard once, followed by a sparse scatter of fine scallion. Surface remains unbroken except the gentle sauce sheen.
- Cut to a calm 60mm final bowl hero. White porcelain spoon rests beside the finished custard while steam fades softly behind it. End with the silky surface and scallion sharp, no other breakfast dishes entering frame.
