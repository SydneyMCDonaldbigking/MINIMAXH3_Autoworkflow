# Silky Steamed Egg Custard — No-Person Production Bible

## Food logic

Whisk eggs with light soy sauce, slowly add warm water while stirring, strain and remove foam for a smooth texture, cover the heatproof bowl and steam gently. Finish the set custard with light soy sauce, sesame oil and scallion; it must have a mirror-smooth surface without bubbles.

## Visual rule

No visible person. The commercial uses ingredients, cookware, steam and only cropped unidentifiable hands when a physical action must be shown. The opening reference uses the physical tabletop sign; H3 prompts do not name it.
