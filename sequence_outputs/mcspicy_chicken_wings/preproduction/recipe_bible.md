# Recipe Bible

Project: McSpicy-Style Crispy Chicken Wings

## Dish Logic

Classic homemade spicy crispy chicken wings inspired by McSpicy-style fried wings. The visual story is raw wings to spicy marinade, then flour press, water dip, second flour press, craggy scale coating, hot oil fry, and red-golden crispy hero. Do not use any restaurant logo or packaging.

## Ingredients

- chicken wing mid-joints and drumettes
- salt
- garlic powder
- onion powder
- white pepper
- paprika
- cayenne or chilli powder
- optional five-spice
- light soy sauce or cooking wine for marinade
- egg or water for wet binder, optional
- all-purpose flour
- cornstarch
- baking powder, optional
- neutral frying oil

## Visual Rules

- Wings must stay as recognizable wing pieces, not nuggets or boneless strips.
- Coating should form craggy crispy scales after pressing flour and dipping water.
- Finished wings should be red-golden and crisp, not sauced buffalo wings.
- No McDonald's, KFC, fast-food packaging, logos, uniforms, branded boxes, or printed copy.
- Keep oil frying realistic and safe; use a deep pot or wok, not shallow burning oil.

## Three Clip Chain

1. Raw wings are seasoned and coated with spicy marinade.
2. Wings are pressed into flour, dipped briefly, pressed again, and the craggy coating appears.
3. Wings fry until red-golden and crisp, then drain and land on a clean serving tray.
