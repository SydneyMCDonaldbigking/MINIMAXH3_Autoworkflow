# Soup Dumplings (Xiaolongbao) — Recipe Sources

- [Directions for Me: pork soup dumplings](https://directionsforme.org/product/233857) — Frozen soup dumplings are spaced on steamer paper or cabbage leaf and steamed at high heat for 9–11 minutes; cook thoroughly.
- [Dim Sum Central: steamed soup dumplings](https://www.dimsumcentral.com/steamed-soup-dumplings/) — Soup dumplings are steamed in a bamboo steamer for about 8–10 minutes and served immediately.
- [USDA safe temperature chart](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) — Pork-filled dumplings must be cooked thoroughly; avoid unsafe handling claims in creative copy.
