# Recipe Bible: Lava Custard Mooncakes

## Production Use

This is a product-selling storyboard, not a baking tutorial. The ad should make
the viewer understand the product: small lava custard mooncakes with golden
salted egg yolk custard filling, sold as a giftable tea-table dessert.

## Visual Food States

- Product reference: round golden mini mooncakes, pressed pattern, no readable brand.
- Gift box state: generic premium gift box with mooncakes in paper cups or tray.
- Character scene: new no-face product presenter in a darker modern kitchen or tea counter, different from the prior beige-apron cook.
- Cut reveal state: one mooncake cut open slowly, orange lava custard visible.
- Tea pairing state: mooncake plate with amber tea, glass teapot, calm table.
- Hero state: gift box, whole mooncakes, one cut-open piece, tea, optional small physical AGO/UMALL card.

## Storyboard

1. Clip 01: gift box hook, mooncake lifted from tray, tea-table setup.
2. Clip 02: slow knife cut and lava custard reveal.
3. Clip 03: final selling hero with gift box, tea, and cut-open mooncake.

## Motion Rules

Use slow commercial motion. One hand action per shot. No fast knife cuts, no
spinning table, no product flying, no camera shake. The custard reveal should
hold still long enough for texture to read.
