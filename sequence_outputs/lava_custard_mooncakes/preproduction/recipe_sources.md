## Production Facts Extracted

- Lava custard should read as salted egg yolk custard: golden orange, glossy, buttery, slightly molten.
- The selling proof is the cut-open mooncake with a soft lava center, not a full recipe process.
- Mooncakes should be small round giftable pastries with a pressed floral/Chinese-style pattern, but no copied logo or readable brand text.
- Serving context: premium gift box, tea pairing, clean tabletop, Mid-Autumn gifting mood.
- Motion should stay slow: unbox, lift, cut, reveal, pour tea, final hero. No fast knife motion or multiple hand actions.
