# 港式西多士 — 无真人制作圣经

## Recipe logic

Spread a thin even layer of smooth peanut butter between two thick crust-trimmed white-bread slices; seal the sandwich gently, dip all sides in beaten egg, then shallow-fry until evenly deep golden and crisp without burning. Plate the toast as a tidy thick square, place one small cold butter square on top and add only a restrained condensed-milk drizzle. The centre must show peanut butter, not jam, chocolate, cheese, meat or an overflowing filling.
