# Recipe and Safety Sources

- [The Woks of Life — Sichuan boiled fish](https://thewoksoflife.com/sichuan-boiled-fish-shui-zhu-yu/): supports marinating thin fish slices, cooking soybean sprouts first, lowering fish one piece at a time into broth, then finishing dried chili and Sichuan peppercorns with hot oil.
- [李锦记 — 水煮鱼](https://usa.lkk.com/zh-cn/foodservices/recipes/sichuan-style-boiled-fish): supports cooking the vegetable base, adding fish slices briefly, transferring to a serving dish and finishing with chili/aromatics.
- [Food Standards Australia New Zealand — Food safety basics](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/consumer/prevention-of-foodborne-illness/food-safety-basics): supports clean separation, temperature control and checking that food is thoroughly cooked.
- [Food Standards Australia New Zealand — Food temperature and thermometers](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/business/charities/temperature-control): supports safe temperature control for potentially hazardous food and clean thermometer handling.

The supplied raw-fish and finished-dish photos define visual identity only; the sources above determine the plausible cooking and safety sequence.
