# Recipe sources - braised eggplant with minced pork (肉末茄子)

Researched 2026-08-11 for the UMALL / Asian Grocer Online Instagram ad. Facts are
paraphrased; no source text is reproduced at length.

## Recipe sources

1. **The Woks of Life, "Braised Eggplant with Minced Pork (肉沫茄子)"**
   <https://thewoksoflife.com/braised-eggplant-pork/>
   Production facts taken: the eggplant is cubed and **soaked in salt water for
   about 15 minutes, then squeezed dry**, which makes it cook faster and drink up
   far less oil. Order in the wok is fixed: ginger and garlic about a minute over
   low heat, then chilli another minute, then the bean sauce a minute, then the
   **minced pork, which is browned before anything else joins it**, and only then
   the eggplant. Sauce is soy, oyster sauce and stock; it braises about **15
   minutes over medium heat until the eggplant is tender**. Finished with chopped
   coriander.

2. **Omnivore's Cookbook and Pups with Chopsticks, Yu Xiang eggplant family**
   <https://omnivorescookbook.com/sichuan-eggplant/>
   <https://pupswithchopsticks.com/chinese-eggplant-with-garlic-sauce/>
   Corroborate the alternative professional order, which reaches the same place:
   the eggplant is **cooked first on its own** until the cut faces are lightly
   charred and it just begins to soften, about five minutes, then **lifted out and
   set aside**; the pork is browned in fresh oil until no longer pink; the
   eggplant is returned for the sauce. Both routes agree on the thing that
   matters here: **the pork and the eggplant are never raw in the wok at the same
   time**, and the eggplant is prepared before it meets the sauce.

## Safety source

3. **NSW Food Authority / FSANZ, cooking temperatures**
   <https://www.foodauthority.nsw.gov.au/consumer/food-at-home/cooking-temperatures>
   Minced meat must reach 75 C throughout, higher than the requirement for a
   whole cut, because mincing distributes surface bacteria through the meat. The
   visible cue is that no pink remains anywhere in the crumb before the eggplant
   and sauce go in.

## What this changes about our first version

The 2026-08-11 ad tipped **raw minced pork onto raw eggplant** and cooked them
together, which neither route does and which cannot reach a browned crumb. It
also inherited a separate template fault: `<Picture 3>` is the working-state
photograph, so the wok in the opening beat was rendered **already full of the
finished dish**, and the raw mince was then dumped on top of it. That is the
error the client saw first.

The corrected structure: eggplant salted and squeezed during prep, pork browned
alone in an **empty** hot wok, aromatics, then the eggplant, then the sauce and a
covered braise until the eggplant collapses and goes glossy.
