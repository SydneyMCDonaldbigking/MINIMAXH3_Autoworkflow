Braised eggplant with minced pork, soft enough to fall apart on the chopsticks.

Salt the eggplant and squeeze it dry before it goes anywhere near the wok, so it drinks up sauce instead of oil. And brown the pork on its own first. The two never go in raw together.

Recipe idea:
Chinese eggplant, cut into batons
Salt, for soaking
Minced pork
Garlic and ginger, chopped
Dried chilli
Spring onion
For the sauce: soy sauce, oyster sauce, stock, a little sugar

Soak the cut eggplant in salt water for 15 minutes and squeeze it out. Brown the mince alone until no pink is left; minced meat needs 75 C throughout. Add the eggplant, then the sauce, and braise until it collapses and turns glossy.

Save this for a quiet weeknight dinner.
