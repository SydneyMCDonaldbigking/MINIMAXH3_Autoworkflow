# Chaopai Premium Beef Bone Broth Hot Pot Base — Revision 2 Storyboard

Three independent 5-second vertical H3 R2V clips. Each has three timed shots and uses the supplied source as product authority.

## Clip 1: Broth-base and claypot setup

- 0.0–1.6s — Medium 45mm counter shot. Begin on forest-green-blouse cook, exact source broth pack, tabletop card and empty black clay sandpot over blue flame. She places the pack upright once beside pot and reaches for one inner sachet. End with sachet held above counter.
- 1.6–3.4s — Cut to 70mm ingredient-board insert. She tears open sachet in one deliberate pull while daikon, tofu, jujubes, scallion and beef bones remain arranged. Actual source pack stays rear-frame; end with opened sachet aimed toward pot.
- 3.4–5.0s — Cut to medium claypot angle. She pours sachet contents into hot water in black sandpot and stops; liquid begins turning golden around blue flame. End on first clear golden swirl.

## Clip 2: Clear simmer proof

- 0.0–1.6s — Close 55mm sandpot shot. Begin on clear golden liquid as cook adds daikon and tofu in one calm motion, keeping broth transparent. Claypot remains centred; end when pieces settle.

- 3.4–5.0s — Cut to medium ladle action. Cook lifts one ladle of clear golden broth above claypot, holds once, then returns it toward pot. End on falling clear ripples.

## Clip 3: Sandpot serving finish

- 0.0–1.6s — Medium 50mm claypot hero continuing from ripples. Cook places fresh scallion at pot edge in one finishing action; beef bone, daikon, tofu and jujubes remain visible.
- 1.6–3.4s — Cut to 75mm close insert. Ladle lifts clear broth and one tender beef piece, holding above pot so texture and golden liquid remain sharp. Tiny 3 cm push-in; end with ladle level.
- 3.4–5.0s — Cut to stable three-quarter final sandpot shot. Ladle returns and cook rests her hand beside pot, leaving exact source pack secondary on rear counter. End on clear steam and complete claypot.
