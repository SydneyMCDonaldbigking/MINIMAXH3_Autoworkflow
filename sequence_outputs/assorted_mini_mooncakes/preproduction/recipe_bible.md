# Assorted Mini Mooncakes — Revision 2 Production Bible

## Creative route

a late-afternoon outdoor garden terrace with a striped canvas umbrella, pale timber dessert table, ceramic tea ware and leafy garden bokeh. Four fictional Asian adult friends: a butter-yellow linen-shirt woman, navy-overshirt man, coral-cardigan woman and cream-knit-polo man. They are dessert guests, not chefs.

## Product and food rules

These are ready-to-serve mini mooncakes. The story proves pressed pastry, colourful assortment and dense filling; it does not invent baking. The user-supplied source is the product authority. No retail package is supplied. Do not invent a carton, pouch or wrapper.

## Reference-image rule

The opening reference visibly includes the supplied product/package where one exists, plus the approved physical tabletop sign. The H3 direction itself does not name that sign.
