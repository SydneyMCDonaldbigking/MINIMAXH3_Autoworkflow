# Assorted Mini Mooncakes — Revision 2 Storyboard

Three independent 5-second vertical H3 R2V clips. Each has three timed shots and uses the supplied source as product authority.

## Clip 1: Garden-table welcome

- 0.0–1.6s — Wide 35mm eye-level terrace shot. Begin on the settled garden dessert table as all four friends take their places; the butter-yellow-shirt woman slides the platter ten centimetres toward centre. Locked camera, tiny 4 cm push-in; end with each pastel pastry readable.
- 1.6–3.4s — Cut to a 70mm table-height close insert. A coral-cardigan hand selects one intact yellow mooncake with a dessert fork while the navy-overshirt friend pours amber tea behind it. One clean action, shallow focus on the pressed pastry; end with the mooncake lifted above the plate.
- 3.4–5.0s — Cut to a medium two-person reaction with the table foreground sharp. The yellow-shirt friend sets the selected mooncake beside a tea cup and the guests lean in naturally. End on the composed platter for the next beat.

## Clip 2: Shared filling reveal

- 0.0–1.6s — Medium 50mm table-side shot from the settled platter. The butter-yellow-shirt friend steadies one yellow mooncake and makes one controlled downward cut with a small dessert knife. Keep the other pastries undisturbed; end when the two halves separate.
- 1.6–3.4s — Cut to an 85mm macro insert. Dense orange filling and thin patterned skin stay sharp as a tea cup is set down softly nearby. Locked-off camera; no new food enters; end on the glossy cut surface.
- 3.4–5.0s — Cut to an over-table medium shot. The coral-cardigan friend accepts one half on a small plate and lifts it once toward the group, while the other guests remain in the terrace setting. End on the shared dessert-table arrangement.

## Clip 3: Four-friend tea-time finish

- 0.0–1.6s — Wide 40mm terrace shot. Begin with all four friends continuing the same tea gathering around the open platter. The navy-overshirt friend pours one short stream of tea into a cup, then stops.
- 1.6–3.4s — Cut to a 75mm close hero. A cream-knit-polo hand lifts one intact supplied mooncake above the platter while cut halves, tea and fruit remain softly visible. Tiny rack focus from cup to pastry; end with its pattern crisp.
- 3.4–5.0s — Cut back to a medium group hero. The mooncake returns to the platter and the four friends bring their cups together once without blocking the food. Locked-off warm outdoor finish.
