# Assorted Mini Mooncakes — Recipe Sources

- [Gelson's Mini Mooncakes](https://www.gelsons.com/recipes/view/mini-mooncakes) — Mini mooncakes use a thin pastry skin around a firm bean-paste filling and are pressed in a mould before baking.
- [Sift & Simmer mini Cantonese mooncakes](https://www.siftandsimmer.com/mini-cantonese-style-mooncakes/) — Mini mooncakes are commonly served with tea and can have dense bean, custard, taro or fruit-style fillings.
- [Hong Kong Centre for Food Safety](https://www.cfs.gov.hk/english/whatsnew/whatsnew_sfst/whatsnew_sfst_mooncake_02092008.html) — Chilled snowy or ice-cream mooncakes need cold storage; do not imply unsafe room-temperature storage for a chilled product.
