# 牛肉夹馍 — 无真人制作圣经

## Recipe logic

Use the supplied product photo as the visual authority for the finished bread: it is a round, layered, pan-toasted flaky flatbread rather than a soft western bun. Start with already braised tender beef chopped into uneven 8–12 mm short pieces and fresh 8–10 mm green-pepper pieces. Sear the beef in a very hot black wok, add green pepper only when the beef is fragrant, then drizzle a small amount of light soy sauce around the hot wok edge and toss quickly until it reduces to a thin brown glaze. The peppers stay bright, crisp-tender and distinct; the beef stays chunky and moist, never minced, puréed or soupy. Toast the flatbread until golden, crisp and layered, split it partially, then fill it while the bread remains visibly flaky.
