# 办公室茯苓百合美苓粥 (Poria Lily Beauty Congee, Office Breakfast) — Production Bible

## Production use

Commercial reference-generation package: three independent 5-second vertical MiniMax H3 Ref2VA clips, no final concat by default.

## Real basis

Tear open one single-serve sachet of the pale ivory powder blend (poria, lily bulb, white fungus, yam, lotus seed, job's tears, soybean and rice, per the supplied packaging) and pour its contents into an empty mug. Pour hot water in a controlled stream over the powder, then stir in slow circles until every dry lump dissolves and the mixture turns smooth, glossy and evenly pale ivory, thick enough to coat the back of a spoon. This is a reconstituted instant product, not a simmered congee: no stovetop or boiling step is shown.
