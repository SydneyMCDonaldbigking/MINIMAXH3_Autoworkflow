# Recipe Bible

Project: Yellow Chive Pork Strips

## Dish Logic

Home-style Chinese yellow chive pork stir-fry. The visual story is delicate pale yellow chives plus tender pork strips. Compared with garlic chive flowers, this dish should feel softer, lighter, and more Cantonese: yellow chives are added late and cooked only briefly.

## Ingredients

- yellow chives
- pork loin or lean pork shoulder, thin strips
- light soy sauce
- oyster sauce
- Shaoxing wine
- white pepper
- cornstarch
- neutral oil
- ginger
- optional scallion

## Visual Rules

- Yellow chives must be pale yellow to light yellow-green, long and delicate.
- Pork must be thin strips, not minced pork, pork belly slabs, or chunks.
- Yellow chives should not darken, collapse, or become green garlic chive flowers.
- Sauce should be light and glossy, not dark heavy soy sauce and not soup.
- Keep unrelated seafood, poultry, dumplings, and drink visuals out of this stir-fry sequence.

## Three Clip Chain

1. Yellow chives and pork strips are prepared; pork is velvetted and chives are cut.
2. Pork strips sear in a hot wok; sauce deglazes and lightly thickens.
3. Yellow chives are added at the end, tossed briefly, and plated as a pale glossy hero.
