## Creative Rules

- This is a separate dish from the garlic chive flower pork strips.
- Keep yellow chives pale yellow to light yellow-green; no buds and no dark green garlic chive flowers.
- Keep pork as thin strips and avoid minced pork, pork chunks, chicken, seafood, dumplings, drinks, or soup.
- Three clips: calm prep and velvet pork, slow controlled wok fold, pale glossy plated hero with rice.
- Motion rule for tomorrow rerun: no fast stir-fry, no wok shaking, no airborne food. Use locked-off shots, two slow broad spatula pushes, one low yellow-chive pour, one gentle fold, and a still final beat.
