# Recipe Sources

Project: Yellow Chive Pork Strips

Target: Australia / Instagram Reels material package.

Sources checked:

- The Woks of Life, Stir-fried Chinese Yellow Chives with Pork: https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-yellow-chives-pork/
- Simply Asian, Pork and Yellow Chives Stir Fry: https://www.simplyasianhome.com/pork-yellow-chives-stir-fry/
- Xiachufang, Yellow Chives with Pork Strips: https://www.xiachufang.com/recipe/105979529/

Working recipe direction:

- Pork strips are velvetted or lightly marinated with water, cornstarch, oyster sauce, and neutral oil.
- Yellow chives are washed, drained well, and cut into 5-6 cm lengths.
- Pork is seared first in a hot wok; sauce is added and thickened lightly.
- Yellow chives enter at the end and cook briefly, because they are delicate.
- Final dish should be pale yellow-green, glossy, tender, and not overcooked.
