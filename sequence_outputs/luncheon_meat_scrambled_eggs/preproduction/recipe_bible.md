# 午餐肉炒鸡蛋 — 无真人制作圣经

## Recipe logic

Cut the supplied luncheon meat into 1.5 cm irregular chunky cubes, pan-fry them first until a portion of the faces has a light golden-brown sear, then set them aside. Beat eggs with a small splash of milk or water and a few scallion rings. Cook over low-to-medium heat, folding only until soft glossy curds form; return the browned meat at the end so both ingredients stay distinct. Avoid a perfectly uniform dice, dry rubbery egg, burnt meat, breading, sauce puddles, rice, toast or unrelated breakfast dishes.
