# 家常双皮奶 — 无真人制作圣经

## Recipe logic

Heat full-fat milk until steaming but not hard boiling, pour it into one pale celadon bowl and let it cool undisturbed to form the first delicate milk skin. Gently pour the milk out from one side while keeping that skin in the bowl. Add separated clear egg white and sugar to the drained warm milk, gently beat only until fully homogeneous and never foamy, then pass it through a fine sieve. Slowly return the strained mixture along the inner bowl wall under the first skin so the skin floats back to the surface. Tightly cover the bowl with clear cling film, prick only two or three tiny steam holes, then steam it in the same compact stainless pot until softly set. Let it cool, then finish with one small dab of jam spread thinly across the centre of the intact second skin. The final dessert has a delicate intact second skin, an ivory silky surface, no bubbles, cracks, scrambled egg, red bean, boba or artificial gloss — only the one restrained jam accent (2026-08-31: recipe updated per user instruction and confirmed against two real jam-double-skin-milk sources, see `recipe_sources.md`; this replaces the earlier "served plain" rule).
