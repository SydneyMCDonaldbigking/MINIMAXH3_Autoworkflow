# Recipe and Safety Sources

- [Great British Chefs — Salmon nigiri](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/recipes/salmon-nigiri-recipe): supports shaping seasoned sushi rice into rounded oblong pieces and draping salmon over the top.
- [RecipeTin Japan — Seared salmon nigiri](https://japan.recipetineats.com/seared-salmon-nigiri-aburi-salmon/): supports forming rice firmly without making it hard and sizing salmon to cover the rice top and sides. This production remains raw, not seared.
- [Food Standards Australia New Zealand — Raw fish dishes](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/consumer/prevention-of-foodborne-illness/raw-fish-dishes): supports buying sashimi-grade fish from a reputable supplier, keeping it cold and maintaining strict hygiene.
- [NSW Food Authority — Seafood safety](https://www.foodauthority.nsw.gov.au/consumer/keeping-food-safe/summer-eating/seafood-safety): supports using fresh, high-quality sashimi-grade fish for sushi and keeping seafood cold.
- [NSW Food Authority — Sushi](https://www.foodauthority.nsw.gov.au/consumer/special-care-foods/sushi): supports the core definition of sushi as acidified cooked rice topped with ingredients such as raw fish and reinforces hygiene controls.

These sources guide physical plausibility and food-safety depiction. The supplied salmon photo is treated only as a visual texture/color reference, not as preparation instructions.
