# 无籽蜜瓜牛奶 — 无真人制作圣经

## Recipe logic

Use only ripe orange cantaloupe flesh. Before the first frame, remove the rind, every seed, the entire seed cavity and all fibrous pale membranes; none may be visible in any ingredient, blender, spill, reflection, garnish or background. Blend 250 g clean melon flesh with 250 ml cold milk and a few ice cubes until fully smooth. The colour should be a natural pale creamy melon-orange, not neon orange, green, grey, speckled, foamy or smoothie-thick. Serve simply, with a small clean seedless orange melon cube garnish if desired.
