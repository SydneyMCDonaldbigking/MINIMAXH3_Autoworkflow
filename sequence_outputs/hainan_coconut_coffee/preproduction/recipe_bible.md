## Recipe logic

Build the drink in a tall chilled clear glass, empty at the start: add clear ice cubes, pour strong cold espresso over the ice, pour the supplied coconut milk in, then stir until the coffee and coconut milk blend into one even light-brown colour throughout. The finished drink must be cold, glossy and drinkable, evenly coloured rather than layered, muddy or artificially striped (2026-08-31: recipe updated per user instruction - build order now starts from an empty glass and ends stirred/blended rather than left as an unmixed gradient; this supersedes the "distinct but natural... layers" line below where the two disagree).
