# Recipe Bible

Project: Luffa Egg Soup

## Dish Logic

Classic Chinese luffa egg soup. The visual identity is ridged green luffa outside, pale green tender pieces after peeling, and yellow egg ribbons or soft cooked egg pieces in a light clear-to-milky soup. The soup should feel clean and homestyle, not thick stew.

## Ingredients

- fresh Chinese luffa or si gua
- eggs
- ginger slices
- scallion
- salt
- white pepper, optional
- sesame oil
- neutral oil or a small amount of pork fat, optional
- hot water or light stock
- goji berries, optional garnish

## Visual Rules

- Luffa must look like Chinese ridged luffa, not cucumber or zucchini.
- Peeled luffa pieces should be pale green and tender, not dark mush.
- Egg should be yellow ribbons or soft cooked pieces, not scrambled dry bits floating randomly.
- Soup should stay light and fresh; no tomato, noodles, meat, seafood, or heavy sauce.
- Keep the pot and bowl clean, bright, and home-style.

## Three Clip Chain

1. Fresh luffa is peeled and cut; eggs are beaten.
2. Ginger and luffa cook briefly; hot water or stock enters and the egg is added.
3. The soup turns fragrant and light; it is ladled into a bowl with scallion and optional goji.
