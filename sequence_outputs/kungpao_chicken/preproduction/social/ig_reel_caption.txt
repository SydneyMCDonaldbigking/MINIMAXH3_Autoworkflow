Kung Pao chicken the way it gets made at home, with diced cucumber and carrot in it.

Mix the sauce before you light the wok, bloom the dried chillies and Sichuan peppercorns in the oil before anything else goes in, and keep the peanuts out until the heat is off. Carrot needs a minute, cucumber needs seconds.

Recipe idea:
Chicken, diced and marinated with corn starch
Carrot, diced
Cucumber, diced
Roasted peanuts
Whole dried red chillies
Sichuan peppercorns
Garlic, ginger, spring onion
For the sauce: light and dark soy, black rice vinegar, Shaoxing wine, sugar, corn starch

Chicken should reach 75 C, with no pink left anywhere. The sauce thickens in about 20 seconds once it hits the wok. Peanuts go in last, off the heat, or they lose their crunch.

Save this one for a weeknight.
