# Kung Pao Chicken with Cucumber and Carrot (宫保鸡丁, home style)

## Production Use

This recipe bible is for a vertical food commercial built around diced chicken,
diced cucumber and carrot, bloomed dried chillies, a pre-mixed sauce, and peanuts
added off the heat.

**Client direction, 2026-08-11: this ad uses the home-style version with cucumber
and carrot.** That is the version sold to this market. It is a real and common
variant but it is not the Sichuan original, so the technique below is taken from
the classic and the ingredient list from the home-style.

## Real Recipe Basis

Three ordering facts drive the whole ad, and our first version broke all three:

1. **The sauce is mixed in a bowl before the wok is lit.** That is how the
   vinegar-sugar balance is controlled; it cannot be improvised into a hot wok.
2. **The dried chillies and Sichuan peppercorns go into the oil first**, before
   any other ingredient, and come out fragrant and darkened. That is where the
   dish's aroma comes from.
3. **The peanuts go in last, off the heat.** Every source warns about this
   explicitly: cook them into the sauce and they go soft.

Plus one from the home-style variant: **carrot before cucumber.** Carrot needs a
minute to soften; cucumber is mostly water and turns limp and grey if it goes in
early, so it needs only seconds.

The chicken is velveted - cut into bite-sized cubes and mixed with corn starch,
water and a little sesame oil until no liquid is visible. It is stir fried until
it has completely lost its pink. The sauce thickens in about 20 seconds once it
hits the wok, and the heat comes off immediately.

Chicken must reach 75 C internally.

## Recipe

Serves: 3

Ingredients:

- 300 g chicken, cut into 1.5 cm dice
- 1 tbsp corn starch, 1 tbsp water, 1 tsp sesame oil, for the marinade
- 100 g carrot, diced to match the chicken
- 100 g cucumber, diced to match, skin left on
- 60 g roasted peanuts
- 8-10 whole dried red chillies
- 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns
- 2 garlic cloves and 1 tsp ginger, chopped; 2 spring onions, cut short
- Sauce, mixed in a bowl beforehand: 1 tbsp light soy, 1 tsp dark soy, 1 tbsp
  black rice vinegar, 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine, 2 tsp sugar, 1 tsp corn starch,
  2 tbsp water

Method:

1. Mix the chicken with corn starch, water and sesame oil until no liquid is
   visible. Set aside.
2. Mix every sauce ingredient in a bowl. Stir again just before it goes in.
3. Dice the carrot and cucumber to the same size as the chicken. Keep them in
   **separate** piles.
4. Heat oil in the wok. Add the dried chillies and Sichuan peppercorns and fry
   only until fragrant and darkened.
5. Add the chicken. Stir fry about 30 seconds, then add garlic, ginger and spring
   onion. Continue until the chicken has completely lost its pink.
6. Add the carrot and fry until it softens. Then add the cucumber, which needs
   only a moment.
7. Stir the sauce and pour it in. It thickens in about 20 seconds. Turn off the
   heat.
8. Stir in the peanuts last, off the heat, and serve.

## Visual Food States

- Product state: raw chicken on a dark plate, no packaging in frame.
- Prep state: marinated chicken glossy with starch, and beside it **two separate
  piles** of bright orange carrot dice and pale green cucumber dice cut to match,
  plus roasted peanuts, whole dried chillies with Sichuan peppercorns, and a bowl
  of dark pre-mixed sauce with a spoon standing in it.
- Bloom state: whole dried chillies and peppercorns darkening in hot oil, sharp
  fragrant smoke lifting, nothing else in the wok.
- Cook state: chicken dice opaque and gold-edged among blackened chillies, orange
  carrot softening among them. **Cucumber not yet added, peanuts not yet added.**
- Glaze state: the pre-mixed sauce poured in and thickening from thin and dark to
  glossy, clinging to every piece, cucumber still bright and crisp.
- Hero state: a shallow bowl of glossy dark-glazed chicken with carrot and
  cucumber still distinct in colour, whole dried chillies and roasted peanuts
  sitting **on top** rather than buried, spring onion scattered, rice beside it.

## Commercial Beat Chain

Clip 01: product hook, the diced components gathered into separate piles beside
the already-mixed sauce, then the dried chillies and Sichuan peppercorns tipped
into the hot oil first, before anything else.

Clip 02: the marinated chicken goes in among the bloomed chillies and turns
opaque, carrot follows and softens, cucumber goes in last and stays crisp, the
pre-mixed sauce is poured in and thickens in seconds.

Clip 03: tipped into the bowl, peanuts scattered last across the surface so they
stay whole and dry on top of the glaze, one glazed cube lifted with chopsticks.
