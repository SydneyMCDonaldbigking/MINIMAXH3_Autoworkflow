# Sau Tao Taiwanese Knife-Cut Noodles — Revision 2 Storyboard

Three independent 5-second vertical H3 R2V clips. Each has three timed shots and uses the supplied source as product authority.

## Clip 1: Package and ingredient setup

- 0.0–1.6s — Medium 45mm counter shot. Start on the indigo-shirt cook beside the exact source noodle pack, small tabletop card and ingredient board. He opens the top seal once and pulls out one compact bundle of broad dry noodles. Camera locked-off; end with the bundle over the board.
- 1.6–3.4s — Cut to a 65mm overhead insert. The cook spreads the wide wavy noodles beside bok choy, scallion, braised egg and minced-pork sauce, then picks the bundle up once with chopsticks. The original pack stays rear-frame; end on dry ribbons ready to cook.
- 3.4–5.0s — Cut to medium hob height. He lowers the compact bundle into clearly boiling water in the black enamel pot over the blue flame. One controlled lowering action; end at water entry under a small plume of steam.

## Clip 2: Boil and noodle lift

- 0.0–1.6s — Close 55mm hob shot beginning at water entry. Broad ribbons loosen naturally in a rolling boil while the cook makes one gentle chopstick separation. Stable horizon; end with distinct wide ribbons moving in water.
- 1.6–3.4s — Cut to an 80mm pot-height insert. The cook lifts one cooked portion above the pot; water drains back in thin streams while flat wavy edges remain visible. Tiny 3 cm push-in only; end with glossy bundle suspended.
- 3.4–5.0s — Cut to medium counter transfer. He guides the lifted noodles into the waiting white bowl next to sauce and vegetables, stopping after one transfer. End on noodles settled in the bowl.

## Clip 3: Sauced-bowl finish

- 0.0–1.6s — Medium 50mm bowl shot. Begin on cooked noodles in the white bowl; the cook adds one spoonful of minced-pork sauce to centre without repetitive stirring. Bok choy and egg remain at the edge; end with sauce visible.
- 1.6–3.4s — Cut to a 75mm close insert. Long chopsticks mix once and lift a small sauced bundle; glossy wide noodles remain recognisable and steam rises behind them. Locked camera, finish on texture.
- 3.4–5.0s — Cut to a calm 60mm bowl hero. Lifted noodles return and the cook sets chopsticks across the rim in one final movement. Exact source pack remains secondary on rear counter; end on complete bowl.
