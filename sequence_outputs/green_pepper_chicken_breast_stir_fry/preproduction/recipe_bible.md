# Green Pepper Chicken Breast Stir-Fry — No-Person Production Bible

## Food logic

Cut green bell pepper into 3 cm irregular medium diamond-shaped chunks, never thin strips. Slice chicken breast thinly across the grain into intentionally varied 2 to 5 cm tapered pieces—some narrow and some wider, with irregular natural edges—so it never reads as identical copied pieces. It must remain visibly plain chicken with no breadcrumb, flour, batter, crust or deep-fried texture. Stir-fry the pepper chunks first until just softened with light browning, add garlic and ginger briefly, then chicken until opaque; finish with a light sauce and toss only until glazed.

## Visual rule

No visible person. The commercial uses ingredients, cookware, steam and only cropped unidentifiable hands when a physical action must be shown. The opening reference uses the physical tabletop sign; H3 prompts do not name it.

2. Prep — Top three-quarter no-person layout: plain chicken breast sliced with deliberately varied natural widths and tapered irregular ends, visible natural muscle grain, medium green bell-pepper chunks, garlic, ginger and a small bowl of light soy sauce. 3. Cooking — Close no-person wok image: opaque, tender, plain chicken-breast slices of mixed natural shapes and thicknesses sit with bright green 3 cm pepper chunks, garlic and ginger. A few chicken pieces carry gentle pale-gold edge colour while others remain lighter, so the dish has real wok variation. Sauce is light and glossy, never a dark heavy stew. Chicken is stir-fried, not breaded, battered, deep-fried or crunchy.
4. Hero — Final no-person plated hero: an intentionally irregular mix of tender glossy unbreaded chicken-breast pieces—varied 2 to 5 cm tapered slices, different widths, occasional pale-gold wok-kissed edges—sits with bright medium green pepper chunks on a white oval plate. A light sauce sheen and sparse scallion; no rice mound, no extra meats, no people, no breadcrumb or fried crust.
