# Classic Wagyu Sukiyaki — No-Person Production Bible

## Food logic

For a classic Kansai-style visual route, use loosely folded, irregular 1 mm Wagyu slices rather than thick rectangular slabs or steak. Sear the thin beef, add sugar and the soy-sake-mirin sauce, then simmer until every visible beef surface is fully cooked warm chestnut-brown with no pink or red remaining; keep it thin and tender rather than dry. Simmer tofu, mushrooms, napa cabbage, long onion and shirataki gently. The sauce must remain a clear, shallow glossy brown warishita, never a broth-heavy hotpot.

## Visual rule

No visible person. The commercial uses ingredients, cookware, steam and only cropped unidentifiable hands when a physical action must be shown. The opening reference uses the physical tabletop sign; H3 prompts do not name it.

## States

1. Opening — Vertical no-person Japanese-table opening. A shallow black cast-iron sukiyaki pan sits on a compact burner over dark walnut. Around it are separate, irregular 1 mm Wagyu slices that are soft pink-red and naturally fine-marbled rather than thick steak-like slabs, with tofu, napa cabbage, shiitake, enoki, Japanese long onion, shirataki and a small ceramic pitcher of dark sweet-savoury sauce. The exact AGO sign from Image 1 is a small physical tent card far back on the table.
2. Prep — Top three-quarter no-person ingredient layout: real 1 mm Wagyu slices overlap loosely like delicate shabu-shabu ribbons on a plate, with small natural flecks of fat rather than exaggerated white lattice marbling; tofu blocks, napa cabbage, shiitake with decorative cuts, enoki, long onion, shirataki and a small bowl of soy-sugar-mirin-sake sauce. Shallow iron pan is empty and ready; no faces or bodies.
3. Cooking — Close no-person shallow-pan image: a few 1 mm Wagyu slices curl naturally at their edges and are fully cooked through in clear, shallow glossy brown warishita. Each visible surface is warm chestnut-brown with natural folded ripples and no pink or red remaining; never thick blocks, grey slabs or a pile of meat. Tofu, shiitake, long onion and napa cabbage simmer around it in distinct zones; sauce is transparent and reduced, never a red soup.
4. Hero — Final no-person dinner hero: shallow iron pan contains a small number of fully cooked loosely folded 1 mm Wagyu ribbons, uniformly warm chestnut-brown but still tender, with tofu, shiitake, napa cabbage, long onion and shirataki in distinct zones of shallow sweet-savoury glaze. No chopstick lift, no people and no unrelated hotpot ingredients.
