Salt and pepper prawns, shells on and fried until they crackle.

Two things make this dish and both happen late. Pat the prawns bone dry and dust them in corn starch before they hit the oil, and keep the wok clean while they fry. The garlic and chilli only go in after the prawns come out.

Recipe idea:
Whole prawns, shell on
Corn starch
Frying oil
Garlic, chopped
Ginger, shredded
Red chilli, sliced
Spring onion
Sea salt, dry roasted
Sichuan peppercorn, ground

Fry at 190 C for about 90 seconds, until the shells go orange and crisp. Pour off the oil to a thin film, bloom the aromatics in what is left, then return the prawns and toss with the salt and pepper. Serve immediately while crisp.

Save this for when you want something loud and fast.
