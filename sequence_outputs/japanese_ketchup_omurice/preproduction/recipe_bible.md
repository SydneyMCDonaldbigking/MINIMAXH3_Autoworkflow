# 日本番茄酱蛋包饭 — 无真人制作圣经

## Recipe logic

Cook small 5 mm chicken-thigh pieces, onion and mushroom, add cold cooked rice, then briefly fry ketchup and finish with butter so the grains become glossy orange-red but remain distinct. Shape the rice as a tidy low oval. Cook a thin smooth omelette just set at the edge and tender in the centre, wrap it around the rice, then add a single simple ketchup ribbon with no writing. Do not use an overstuffed omelette, torn egg, red sauce puddle, runny raw egg, western cheese filling or decorative ketchup lettering.
