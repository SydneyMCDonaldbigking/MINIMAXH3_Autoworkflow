# Recipe Bible

Dish name: Tuna Tataki Don

Core ingredients:

- Sashimi-grade tuna loin/block, visually matched to the supplied deep-red tuna reference.
- Neutral oil for quick searing.
- Ponzu, soy sauce, toasted sesame oil, grated ginger, scallion, toasted sesame.
- Steamed short-grain rice.
- Cucumber, avocado, nori, lemon or chili thread as optional garnish.

Visual chain:

1. Product/prep hook: chilled tuna, ponzu sauce kit, rice-bowl components.
2. Heat proof: tuna loin hits a hot pan and sears quickly on the outside while staying rare.
3. Hero payoff: sliced tuna tataki over rice with ginger ponzu drizzle, sesame, scallion, cucumber/avocado.

H3 reference requirements:

- Raw-state reference: deep-red sashimi-grade tuna loin or thick slices, sauce kit, rice components.
- Cook-state reference: seared outside, rare red centre, clean knife slices.
- Hero-state reference: tuna tataki don bowl with glossy ponzu.

Negative identity notes:

- No chicken, no wontons, no soup.
- Do not turn tuna into canned tuna, cooked flaky fish, salmon, beef steak, or poke-only cubes.
- No readable labels, fake prices, subtitles, overlays, or floating logos.
